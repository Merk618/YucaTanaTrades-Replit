# Breakout Radar

**Breakout Radar by YucaTanaTrades — Powered by Meridian OS**

> Know what changed. Understand why it matters. Track what happened next.

## Product direction

Breakout Radar is a focused decision-support service for investors following emerging-growth breakouts. It is designed to turn noisy market movement into a concise, evidence-backed view of:

- what changed;
- why the change may matter;
- what could invalidate the thesis;
- what to watch next; and
- what actually happened afterward.

## Public build objective

The first release is a narrow paid beta centered on a daily Breakout Radar and a transparent weekly outcome review. The product will earn trust through useful evidence, consistent provenance, and an honest record—not through hype or guaranteed-return language.

## Product principles

1. **Evidence before excitement.** Every surfaced item should show its source and timestamp.
2. **Changes over snapshots.** The product emphasizes meaningful changes in setup, not generic rankings.
3. **Risks beside catalysts.** Decision support must show both sides.
4. **Outcomes stay visible.** Prior radar items remain reviewable after the fact.
5. **No execution theater.** Breakout Radar is research software, not autonomous trade execution.
6. **No guaranteed outcomes.** Nothing in the product promises profit or removes market risk.
